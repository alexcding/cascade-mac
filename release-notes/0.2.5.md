### New

- Run and Stop are one button, with a gear beside it for the scheme and run destination. A pick is saved as you make it, so the destination sheet is gone.
- When Run cannot start, it says why under the scheme. A saved device that is unplugged stays chosen rather than the build going somewhere else.
- Next Model (⌘D) and your model presets switch like ⌘Tab: over the terminal a switcher appears at its foot and switches when you let go; in Chat they switch at once. Edit presets in Settings → Shortcuts.
- One model picker everywhere, the chat's own: starred presets and a tab per agent, search, and the effort, Thinking and Auto-compact controls.
- In the terminal, Cascade's Claude status line (when you have none of your own) shows the model, effort, context and the session's cost, with how much of your plan's session and week is left and when each resets, fitted to the terminal's width.

### Improved

- The session toolbar is simpler: Run, the build title and a Terminal / Chat switch. The agent shows its own model and context, in its status line or the chat composer. Compact and Clear are in the Product menu.
- The IDE icon is back beside the scheme and opens the IDE; an IDE without artwork of ours shows its installed app's icon.
- Switching between chats is near instant.
- New Task's chat form lists and names models as the chat does.
- Automation uses Cascade's own controls, and a schedule's time is set to the minute.
- Deleting a project deletes its chats, and removing the last session on a worktree deletes the chats started in its pane. Archive is gone, and sidebar chat rows show their title alone.
- Chat, the diff and Automation sit on the same background as the pane around them.
