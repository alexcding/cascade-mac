### Improved

- Everything inside the app now goes by Cascade: the database is `cascade.db`, and scripts get `CASCADE_*` variables such as `CASCADE_WORKTREE_PATH`, `CASCADE_BRANCH` and `CASCADE_PR_URL`.
- Nothing needs changing on your side. The `CRAFT_*` variables are still set for scripts written earlier, hooks and status lines installed under Craft keep working, and automation pipelines that used Craft's own actions keep running.
