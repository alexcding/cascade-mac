### New

- Projects can be pinned. Right-click a project and choose Pin: it moves into the sidebar's Pinned section with its sessions, and can be dragged among the pinned sessions in any order. A pinned project keeps its folder open or closed as it was.

### Improved

- A project's row shows selected while its Settings, or the New Task opened from its row, is on screen. A click on a project still only opens or closes it.
- Rows show a highlight under the pointer, a step lighter than the selection, and neighbouring highlights keep a small gap between them.
- With every project pinned, the empty Projects heading is hidden.

### Fixed

- Deleting a project removes its sessions and their worktrees too, after one warning, instead of leaving them behind.
