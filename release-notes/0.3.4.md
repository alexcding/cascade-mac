### New

- A paired Mac's sessions show what their agent is doing there: stopped, running, working, or waiting for you.
- Switch a paired Mac's session to Chat, as you can a session on this Mac. Messages you send are typed into its terminal there.
- Right-click a paired Mac's session to rename, start, fork or remove it there, or right-click one of its projects for a New Task there. A session started this way runs on that Mac without taking over its screen.
- Pin a paired Mac's sessions and projects. Pins stay on this Mac, at the top of that Mac's section.

### Improved

- A paired Mac's terminals use this Mac's theme and font.
- A paired Mac's projects show their icons.
- The Simulator follows the pointer while you drag.

### Fixed

- The Simulator takes input again after it was restarted outside Cascade.
