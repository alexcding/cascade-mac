### New

- Fork a session from its right-click menu in the sidebar or from the chat. The fork is a new session with a copy of the worktree, and its agent carries on the same conversation. Forks are marked in the sidebar.

### Fixed

- Clicking a session in the sidebar puts the keyboard in its terminal or chat, so you can start typing straight away. Typing in the sidebar no longer jumps to another row, such as Automation when you pressed "a".
