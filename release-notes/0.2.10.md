### New

- The sidebar's bell is now an activity capsule beside Settings: it shows the latest event until you open it, and unfolds into today's activity, with the full Activity log a click away.
- New installs keep worktrees in one folder, ~/.cascade/worktrees, out of your projects' folders. Existing installs keep making them where they did.
- Cascade offers once to move your sessions' worktrees into that folder: uncommitted changes go with them, and agents pick up their conversations in the new place.
- Settings → Worktrees lists the worktrees in that folder, including ones left behind by a removed project, and deletes each with one click.

### Improved

- Removing a worktree also deletes its branch when Cascade made that branch and it is merged; any other branch is kept. This replaces the "Delete the branch" setting.
