### Improved

- A new app icon and menu bar icon: three parallel bars in place of the folded C.
- Sessions are named for their work, not their branch. A session started from a pull request, issue or Jira ticket takes its title at once; one started from a prompt gets a short title a few seconds after it starts. A name you give it yourself always wins.
- Start a session with nothing typed. It gets a branch of its own, and the agent waits for your first message.
- The sidebar uses the system's own sidebar material, and its icons are a step darker in light mode.

### Fixed

- Chat: a finished Claude subagent task no longer shows as running, and a task cut off by an interrupted turn shows as stopped. Codex subagent threads settle when they finish.
- Chat: Claude's data-loss warnings and refusals now show, while routine notices, early API retries and background-task announcements stay out of the conversation. Codex's routine log output no longer shows as errors.
- Simulator: a key press is never left held down, and the video stream runs only while the pane is on screen.
- A paired Mac watching your terminal can no longer freeze it.
- Closing a pane tab folds it away smoothly instead of the tabs after it jumping into the gap.
